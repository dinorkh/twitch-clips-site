1. Scope and current availability

These terms describe the TwitchTikTok personal software project and this informational website. The software is experimental, under development, and used privately on Windows. It is not currently offered to the public, licensed for download here, or provided as a hosted editing service.

There is no registration, purchase, or account connection on this website. Visiting it does not give access to the desktop tool.

2. Responsibility for content

Anyone using the private tool is responsible for having the rights, permissions, and other legal grounds required to obtain, process, edit, and share source material. Access to a Twitch video alone does not establish permission to reuse it.

Review the rights and privacy interests of streamers, players, speakers, and other people appearing in a clip. Follow applicable law and the rules of the platforms involved. Do not use the tool to bypass access restrictions.

3. Review and future transfers

Candidate scores, transcriptions, subtitles, titles, and boundaries can be incorrect or incomplete. The user must check the content, context, attribution, and final edit.

A TikTok account connection and video transfer feature are planned, not implemented. The intended transfer would require user authorisation and a deliberate selection of the video, followed by completion in TikTok. The tool does not currently upload to or automatically publish on TikTok.

4. Independent project

TwitchTikTok is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or approved by TikTok or Twitch. Platform names are used to describe the intended workflow. Their trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Third-party services have their own terms and policies. This website does not replace them or promise access to their APIs.

5. Experimental software and limitations

The private prototype is provided as is and as available, to the extent permitted by applicable law. No guarantee is made about accuracy, continuity, compatibility, platform acceptance, or editorial results. Features may change or remain unfinished.

Keep independent copies of source files and review exports before relying on them. Nothing in these terms excludes responsibilities or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

6. Operator details and changes

No public operator identity or contact address has yet been provided on this website. An appropriate operator identification and privacy/support contact remain to be supplied before offering the tool to other people.

These pages must be reviewed when the software's availability or data practices change. The date below identifies this version; it is not a promise of ongoing service or support.
