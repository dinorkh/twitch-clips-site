From a long stream

TwitchTikTok is a personal Windows tool for analysing Twitch videos, selecting moments, and preparing subtitled vertical clips. The creator reviews the results before deciding what to share.

Explore the workflow →Privacy & data

A private working prototype, not a public service. There is no sign-up, download, or TikTok connection on this website.

Prepare locally.
Review deliberately.

The workflow helps surface candidates. It does not guarantee that a moment is interesting, accurate, or ready to publish.

01 / FIND

Understand the source

Analyse authorised Twitch videos using local audio signals, existing transcriptions, and lightweight visual checks to propose candidate moments.

02 / REVIEW

Keep the full situation

Review the selection and its boundaries. Gaming, discussions, and longer discussions remain separate categories, with room for humour and quieter exchanges.

03 / PREPARE

Build a vertical version

Prepare a 9:16 composition, preserve the original audio, add synchronised subtitles, and keep proposed titles and descriptions available for manual review.

What exists. What comes next.

Implemented locally

A private editing workflow

  • Video analysis and candidate selection with editorial review.
  • Local clip exports and viewing catalogues.
  • Experimental vertical 9:16 editing with webcam and gameplay.
  • Dynamic subtitles and separate title/description metadata.

These features run in the developer's local prototype. They are not offered as a public application.

Planned · Not implemented

A user-controlled TikTok hand-off

A future connection may use TikTok OAuth and the video.upload permission to send a selected video for completion in the TikTok mobile app.

The intended flow requires the creator to continue editing and finish posting in TikTok. Automatic publication is not part of the plan. API access and implementation are not guaranteed.

This website is informational only. It does not accept uploads, process media, connect accounts, or provide an OAuth callback. Publishing these pages does not constitute TikTok developer approval.

Your content. Your final decision.

Use only content you own or are authorised to process and share. Review subtitles, context, rights, and the final edit before any transfer or publication.
